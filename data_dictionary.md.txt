Data Dictionary

Dataset Overview

Dataset:IBM Telco Customer Churn

Total Records:7,043 customers

Total Columns:21

The dataset contains customer demographic information, subscribed services, account details, billing information and churn status.

Column Descriptions

| Column | Data Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| customerID | TEXT | Unique identifier assigned to each customer. |
| gender | TEXT | Customer's gender. |
| SeniorCitizen | INTEGER | Indicates whether the customer is a senior citizen (1 = Yes, 0 = No). |
| Partner | TEXT | Indicates whether the customer has a partner. |
| Dependents | TEXT | Indicates whether the customer has dependents. |
| tenure | INTEGER | Number of months the customer has remained with the company. |
| PhoneService | TEXT | Indicates whether the customer subscribes to phone service. |
| MultipleLines | TEXT | Indicates whether the customer has multiple phone lines. |
| InternetService | TEXT | Type of internet service subscribed to. |
| OnlineSecurity | TEXT | Indicates whether the customer subscribes to online security. |
| OnlineBackup | TEXT | Indicates whether the customer subscribes to online backup. |
| DeviceProtection | TEXT | Indicates whether the customer subscribes to device protection. |
| TechSupport | TEXT | Indicates whether the customer subscribes to technical support. |
| StreamingTV | TEXT | Indicates whether the customer subscribes to streaming TV. |
| StreamingMovies | TEXT | Indicates whether the customer subscribes to streaming movies. |
| Contract | TEXT | Customer's contract type. |
| PaperlessBilling | TEXT | Indicates whether the customer uses paperless billing. |
| PaymentMethod | TEXT | Payment method used by the customer. |
| MonthlyCharges | REAL | Monthly amount charged to the customer. |
| TotalCharges | REAL | Total amount charged to the customer over the recorded account period. |
| Churn | TEXT | Indicates whether the customer left the company (Yes or No). |

 Data Quality Notes

- The dataset contains 7,043 customer records.
- No duplicate customer IDs were detected.
- Eleven records contain NULL values in `TotalCharges`.
- All eleven records with missing `TotalCharges` have zero tenure and a churn status of `No`.
- The missing values were preserved and not replaced with zero.
